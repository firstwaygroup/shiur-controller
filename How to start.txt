SHIUR - how to start

FIRST TIME ONLY
  Double-click "Install Shiur".
  It copies everything onto this laptop, keeps your settings and list,
  and puts a Shiur icon on your desktop.

EVERY NIGHT
  1. Tap the Shiur icon.
     Everything starts by itself: OBS (hidden), the wall screen,
     your Zoom meeting, and the Shiur app.

  2. The Shiur app has two sizes:
     BIG (ME or SHOW - nobody sees your laptop):
       Zoom tiles on the left, big buttons, tonight's list, Find something.
     STRIP along the bottom (SCREEN, or while you look something up):
       Everyone sees everything above the strip.

     ME        Zoom sees you. The wall shows the title.
     SHOW      The wall and Zoom see the picture or video from the list.
     SCREEN    The wall and Zoom see your screen (not the strip).
     Charts and web pages in the list open live on your screen.
     Tap the Zoom tiles to open Zoom big; tap again to put it back.

  3. Before the shiur: tap Add to fill tonight's list
     (files from the laptop, your recent downloads, or a web address).

  4. At the end, tap End shiur (twice), then end the meeting in Zoom.

IF SOMETHING IS WRONG
  Tap Setup at the top of the app.
