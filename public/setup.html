1Connect to OBS

Checking...
  1. Open OBS. Click Tools, then WebSocket Server Settings.
  2. Tick "Enable WebSocket server". Click "Show Connect Info" and copy the password.
  3. Paste it below and click Save and connect.

2Create the shiur scenes

Sets up OBS for you: you on your own, the item with you beside it, the laptop screen with you beside it, and the wall. Nothing on show is covered. Safe to press again.

3Choose the camera

4Choose the laptop screen (for SCREEN)

This is the screen you work on. When you press SCREEN, everyone sees it, but not the Shiur strip at the bottom or the taskbar: those are cut out automatically when you start with the Shiur icon.

5The wall screen

Plug the TV into the laptop, then pick it here (at home, one of your extra screens). It opens by itself every time from now on.

6Your Zoom meeting

Paste your meeting link. The Shiur icon then opens your meeting by itself, and your Zoom tiles show in the Shiur app.

  1. In Zoom: Settings, Video, Camera: choose OBS Virtual Camera.
  2. Same page: untick "Always show video preview dialog when joining".
  3. Settings, Audio: tick "Automatically join audio by computer when joining a meeting".
  4. In the meeting, choose Gallery view (tiles). Zoom remembers it.

7The start page for finding things

Big buttons for MDY, Claude, Google Images and Sefaria. Tip: in Chrome, set it as the page that opens on start (Settings, On start-up, Open a specific page: http://localhost:8080/start).
